Privacy Policy

Effective November 8, 2026

This policy explains what information the Letter Drop app ("Letter Drop", "we", "us") collects, how it is used and the choices you have. Letter Drop is a word game for iPhone. It does not ask for your real name, email address, contacts, photos or location.

Information stored on your device

Your progress (levels completed, scores, streaks, statistics and settings) is saved on your iPhone. It stays there unless you delete the app.

Information we collect

To run leaderboards and friends, Letter Drop creates an anonymous account the first time you use these features. No email or password is involved. We store:

  • A random account ID that identifies your anonymous account.
  • The display name you choose (3–16 characters). It is shown to other players on leaderboards.
  • Your Daily and Endless scores and the dates you played, to rank you on leaderboards.
  • Friend connections and invite codes, when you invite a friend or accept an invite.

This information is stored with our backend provider, Supabase (supabase.com), and is used only to provide leaderboards and friends. We do not sell it.

Advertising

Letter Drop shows ads from Google AdMob: ads between games, and optional rewarded ads you can choose to watch for a continue or a Daily replay. Google may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Google uses it to show ads, measure them, prevent fraud and, if you allow it, personalize them.

  • iOS asks whether Letter Drop may track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, the advertising identifier is not shared, and ads are not personalized based on tracking. You can change this any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Learn how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases

The optional "Remove Ads" purchase is processed by Apple. We never see your payment details. We only receive confirmation from Apple that the purchase was made, so ads can be turned off.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Apple Game Center, your Daily and Endless scores are submitted to Game Center leaderboards. This is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

Letter Drop is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Keeping and deleting your information

We keep leaderboard and friends data while your account is active. To delete your server data (display name, scores and friend connections), email cashewcampus@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion" and your in-game name. We delete it within 30 days. Deleting the app removes the data stored on your phone.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, the EU, UK or California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to object to certain processing. To make a request, email us at the address below.

Security

Information sent between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.

Contact

Questions or requests: cashewcampus@gmail.com
