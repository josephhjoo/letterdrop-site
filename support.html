Support

Need help, found a bug, or have an idea? Email cashewcampus@gmail.com. We usually reply within two business days. If you can, include your iPhone model, iOS version and the app version (shown at the bottom of Settings).

Common questions

How do I play?
Letters fall one at a time. Drag anywhere to slide the letter left or right. Hold your finger down to drop it faster, flick down to drop it instantly, and swipe up to put it on hold for later. Spell a word of 3 or more letters across, or down, and it clears. Clears can make the letters above fall into new words, setting off cascades. The game ends when the board fills to the top.
I bought Remove Ads. How do I get it back on a new phone?
Open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchase. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with. Remove Ads is a one-time purchase and never needs to be bought again.
What does Remove Ads include?
It removes the ads shown between games for good. Continues and Daily replays that normally need a short ad become free, with no ad.
How do refunds work?
Purchases are handled by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
My Daily streak reset. Why?
Your streak counts consecutive days with a completed Daily puzzle, in your phone's local time. Missing a day starts it over. Your best streak is always kept on the Stats page.
How do friends and leaderboards work?
Tap Invite a friend on the leaderboard to send a link. When your friend opens it in Letter Drop, you are added to each other's Friends list. Scores also appear on Game Center leaderboards if you are signed in to Game Center.
How do I delete my data?
Deleting the app removes everything stored on your phone. To also delete your leaderboard name, scores and friends from our server, email cashewcampus@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion" and your in-game name. We delete it within 30 days. See the Privacy Policy for details.
