Drop letters. Make words.

Letter Drop is a falling-letter word game for iPhone. Steer each letter into place, spell words across and down to clear them, and set off chain-reaction cascades and bombs before the board fills up.

  • Daily puzzle: the same letters for everyone, every day. Keep your streak alive.
  • Endless: play as long as you can, and it speeds up as you go.
  • 100 levels with Mr. Webster, each with its own goal.
  • Summit league: climb weekly leagues and race your friends.
